ASSIGNMENT 1
Prompts Used:

Prompt 1:
Please write a summary of this article.

Prompt 2:
Read this article and write a short summary in exactly 3 sentences. Include the main idea and important points.

Prompt 3:
You are a news expert. Read the article and write a summary that includes the main events, the feeling or tone of the article, and what it means.

Outputs

Prompt 1 Output:
The article explains how climate change is causing more extreme heat, which is affecting people's health, especially older adults. Experts are asking for urgent action, such as reducing fossil fuel use and increasing support for clean energy. The issue remains serious and needs quick solutions to avoid bigger problems in the future.

Prompt 2 Output:
Climate change is leading to more heatwaves, which are harming people's health around the world. Older people are more at risk from the rising temperatures. The article says it's important to act now by cutting fossil fuels and supporting renewable energy.

Prompt 3 Output:
This article highlights growing concerns about climate change and its direct impact on human health. The tone is serious and urgent, urging leaders to take stronger steps to stop fossil fuel use and invest in clean energy. It also warns that failing to act quickly could lead to much bigger problems for future generations.

ASSIGNMENT 2
Prompts Used:

Role-Based Prompt:
You are a hiring manager reviewing a job applicant's resume. Please read and evaluate the following resume. Give feedback on the strengths, weaknesses, and if the resume is a good match for a data analytics role.

Generic Prompt:
Give your thoughts on this resume.

Comparison Report

Quality of Response:

The role-based prompt gave a proper, well-structured review. It mentioned good points like knowledge of Power BI and certifications, and also pointed out areas to improve — like doing more practical projects or learning advanced ML skills.

The generic prompt gave a basic review. It only said the resume is good and mentioned a few skills, but didn’t say how it can be improved.

Tone and Style:

The role-based output sounded professional, just like how a recruiter or hiring manager would speak. It had phrases like “entry-level potential” and “recommended to improve cloud knowledge.”

The generic output was more casual and less formal. It sounded like general feedback without detailed points.
